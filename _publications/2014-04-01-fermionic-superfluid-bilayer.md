We study the possibility of realizing a fermionic superfluid starting from a bilayer band insulator in an optical lattice. Using mean-field theory and beyond mean-field approaches, we analyze the BEC-BCS crossover in this system and show how the interlayer hopping can be tuned to drive a transition from a band insulator to a superfluid phase. Our results provide guidance for cold atom experiments seeking to realize exotic superfluid phases in optical lattice systems.
