Using determinant quantum Monte Carlo (DQMC), we study the interplay of disorder and strong interactions in a bilayer band insulator. We compute the finite-temperature phase diagram and show how disorder modifies the interaction-driven transition, affecting pairing correlations and spectral properties in the bilayer Hubbard model.
