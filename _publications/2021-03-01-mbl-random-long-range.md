We investigate many-body localization (MBL) in a one-dimensional disordered spin chain with random long-range interactions that decay as a power law with distance. Using exact diagonalization, we study the level statistics, participation ratio, and entanglement entropy to map the MBL phase boundary as a function of the interaction range. We find that long-range interactions can both stabilize and destabilize MBL depending on the power-law exponent, providing new insights into MBL in systems with non-local interactions relevant to trapped ion experiments.
